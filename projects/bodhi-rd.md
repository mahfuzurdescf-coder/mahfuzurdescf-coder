# BODHI — Biodiversity Observation and Data for Hyperlocal Intelligence

**Status:** Research and development  
**Field:** Biodiversity informatics and conservation technology

## The idea

Conservation work often has local observations, images, sound recordings, and environmental information that are difficult to combine into structured evidence.

BODHI is being developed as an approach to organise these inputs and support hyperlocal biodiversity analysis. Its research direction includes bioacoustics, species information, habitat context, and reviewable field records.

## Current workstreams

- **Bioacoustics:** workflows for reviewing field recordings affected by distance, low signal levels, and background noise. Candidate detections need confidence information and human review.
- **Species knowledge:** structured scientific and vernacular names, referenced identification features, habitat context, and media attribution.
- **Habitat context:** satellite-derived landscape indicators combined with site observations and validation steps. A remote-sensing signal alone is not treated as proof of species presence or population change.

## Design principles

- Work from traceable observations and documented methods.
- Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a species-level answer.
- Keep expert review central to scientific identification and public claims.
- Treat data provenance, location sensitivity, and the rights to use media as essential parts of quality.

## My contribution

I am developing the research-product direction, workflow concepts, and integration approach between conservation knowledge and digital tools.

## Current boundary

BODHI is not presented as a validated public species-identification model. No unpublished field records, sensitive locations, or private media are included here. Performance, accuracy, and ecological conclusions will be reported only after appropriate testing and expert review.
